# AROW Mobile

**Explore railways. Find a milestone. Simulate your location.**

AROW Mobile brings railway maps and controlled GPS simulation together on
Android. Find a point along a railway and use it as your phone's reported
location to explore railway geolocation scenarios.

Built for railway research and development, AROW stands for **Advanced Railway
geOlocation Workflow**. Mobile is the Android companion to AROW Desktop;
data exchange between the two applications is planned.

## From the map to a simulated position

1. **Explore the network.** Select railway sections and milestones, inspect their
   details, and choose which layers appear on the map.
2. **Find your point.** Search by railway name or line code, choose a section,
   and enter a kilometre point such as **PK 509+000**.
3. **Start a simulation.** Apply the selected point as your phone's location.
   Follow its status in AROW and stop the simulation when you're finished.
